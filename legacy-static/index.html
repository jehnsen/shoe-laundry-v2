FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us at (555) 012-3456 or send a message below.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Book a 2-hour window online. Our driver collects your items in a reusable bag (shoes go in individual protective sleeves) and returns them at your chosen delivery time. It's free on orders over ₱30.

How long does it take?

Wash & fold and pressing are usually ready in 24–48 hours. Dry cleaning and standard shoe cleaning take 48 hours. Restoration and repaint work can take 3–5 days depending on the condition.

Which shoes can you clean?

Sneakers, canvas, leather, suede, nubuck, knit and most boots. We'll assess each pair on arrival and let you know if anything needs special treatment or isn't suitable for cleaning.

What if something gets damaged or lost?

Every item is tagged and photographed at intake and is covered by our insurance while in our care. In the rare event of an issue, we'll make it right under our satisfaction guarantee.

Can you remove every stain?

We treat every stain we find, and most come out completely. Some older or set-in stains may only lighten — we'll always tell you before using stronger treatments on delicate fabrics.

Can I drop off in person instead?

Of course. Our studio is open seven days a week, and walk-in drop-offs receive the same turnaround times as pickup orders.
